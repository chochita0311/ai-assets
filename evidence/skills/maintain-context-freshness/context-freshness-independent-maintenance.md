# Independent context maintenance validation

## Context And Applicability

Evaluate post-creation use of maintain-context-freshness: do approved updates
reach the smallest content owners while unique history, provenance and routes
back to evidence remain available? The skill owns the maintenance method; the
smallest content documents own durable content state. Audit/action tracking stays session-only
unless the user explicitly requests a named durable artifact. This record is
the established owner explicitly selected for retaining the follow-up results;
that request does not authorize audit metadata in the underlying project docs.

The practical question is necessary, accurate maintenance during ordinary use.
An isolated A/B is not required for a bounded field observation. Claims about a
particular revision's effect still need comparable evidence. Reading a skill's
contract or an author's final report alone is not an independent maintain run.

## Evidence And Evaluation

### First usage-history review: 2026-11-01

Earlier revisions retained a planned question with no attached execution
output. A retrospective review now found actual applications; the earlier lack
of attached output did not establish that no usage history existed.

One shared discovery pass inspected 2,423 locally stored session-log files

These are discovery/loading counts, not 116 verified ordinary maintenance

After an initial contract-only inspection was corrected, three read-only

This record owns three maintenance cases. The other two real flows and the two

Nine source packets included a migration parent and two children; those three
packets are one work flow, not three independent cases. Both records describe
one review, not two additional experiments.

The selected observations span 2026-08-07 through 2026-10-27, including the
other record's controls. These are histories preserved on the reviewed PC;
they do not prove that every historical action ran on that physical machine.
Unavailable/deleted histories and other machines are outside coverage. No
representative invocation census or success rate was calculated.

### Identity, preservation and coverage limits

Original private session histories and the originating 2026-11-01 audit
conversation retain source locators, observations and review provenance.
Private paths, raw traces, identities and private-to-public associations are
excluded here. Public readers cannot verify the raw histories from this record
alone. Temporary extraction/analysis files were removed after verification;
they are not evidence owners or dependencies for a later review.

Source Git revisions and whole-package loaded-byte fingerprints were not
established for these historical applications. The subject fields remain null;
current package bytes must not be substituted for past loaded content. A
returned entry body does not prove receipt of every reference or effective
instruction. Source-author/evaluator independence, population-wide quality and
revision-attributed improvement were not established. Some combined outputs
were truncated, although the key patches and relevant source conditions were
available separately.

The review checked document changes, not current production behavior. For M2,
diff/whitespace and 48 relative-path existence checks were observed; that is
not anchor validation or verification of current cluster, TLS or schema state.
No new forward test, runtime operation or full claim-by-claim preservation
audit of every target passage was performed.
