# Harness mapping v10 validation

## Context And Applicability

The improvement question is whether the runtime-neutral source-layer split and
mapping v10 preserve consumer contracts, accepted historical baselines and
record identity while placing current evidence with its responsible asset
owner. The [Harness Adoption Guide](../../../harness/ADOPTION-GUIDE.md) owns
adoption and refresh; the [record contract](../../../harness/policies/improvement-record-contract.md)
owns record placement, identity and admission. This record owns the validation
question, not a second adoption procedure.

The reviewed source separates portable roles, flows, operations, profiles,
workflow policies and templates under `harness/`; adapters, shared instructions
and prompt presets under `agents/`; and common baselines, delegation and review
criteria under `policies/`. The former workflow and runner are now delivery and
delivery-runner. One common orchestrator and workflow governance own selection,
transitions, common authority and resources. Delivery controller inputs,
decisions, outputs and prohibitions moved to execution governance; the separate
research controller's applicable duties were
retained in the common role and research policy before its role file was removed.
Each flow retains its own sequence, lifecycle and acceptance contract.

Mapping v10 retains consumer roots `docs/agents/`, `docs/policies/`,
`docs/plans/` and `docs/evidence/`, with manifest schema 1. Renames, role
retirement and link changes require controlled refresh. The public evidence
collection retains one format, question/record identity space and catalog across
the current `harness`, `agents`, `policies` and `skills` owner domains.
The observing actor or flow and historical subject kind/key do not select a
record's folder. Selected public records map to the same domain under the
consumer's `docs/evidence/`; historical locators remain accepted baseline
evidence until refresh succeeds.

Use this evidence for a selected fresh adoption, historical mapping refresh or
source-placement review. Research utility, runtime discovery, personal skill
installation and independent reviewer effectiveness are separate questions.
The completed evidence here consists of static source checks, manual consumer
rendering and contract rehearsals. No importer, actual consumer migration or
installed manifest was created or executed by those mapping trials.

## Evidence And Evaluation

### Source Identity And Preserved Contracts

The subject fingerprints identify the public source files inspected for this
record, not every file in the historical checks. Their `source_revision` is
null because the assessed content was an uncommitted candidate. A later commit
must not be backdated as its test-time provenance. Historical trial snapshots
were distinct; the counts below are retained for their own snapshots rather
than presented as a fresh inventory of the latest repository.

Research and Harness Improvement remain independent selections. The current
research-only payload contains 13 files: five common dependencies, four research
core files and the complete four-file research-sadr method package. Common
dependencies remain required when shared roles or flows are exported, whether
research is selected or omitted; they do not select a sibling flow or
capability. The method retains its structure, bytes, internal links and declared
version metadata, with separate source revision and source/rendered hashes for
every file. It is loaded directly from the consumer's local `SKILL.md`.
A globally discovered same-name skill cannot substitute for this pinned copy.
Global skill installation and runtime bindings remain separate from harness
adoption. Research does not select the eight-file improvement core, public
records or experience storage; Improvement does not select research or force
delivery documents, artifacts or a delivery controller on other tasks.

The `SOURCE-ONLY` exception is limited to import/refresh administration. Its
target must be the existing file exactly at `harness/ADOPTION-GUIDE.md` in the
selected source snapshot, and any supplied anchor must resolve in that file.
Existence of a directory at that path, a different file, or an invalid anchor
does not meet the condition. Only then may the reference become meaningful
plain text naming the guide, current mapping and administrative use. The
current mapping is resolved from that selected source; an installed manifest's
historical mapping is not rewritten as current. This exception neither adds
the guide to the payload nor permits an external checkout dependency in normal
operation or omission of a required method, governance or flow dependency.

Local modifications and detached `local` entries remain protected. Every
entry's historical source revision, mapping version, source hash and rendered
hash are preserved together until its managed refresh is accepted. A managed
target differing from its recorded rendered hash is protected local drift and
must not be overwritten automatically. Detached local entries retain their
target, original effective source locator and detachment baseline unconditionally;
ordinary refresh cannot advance that baseline, and reattachment requires an
explicit ownership decision. A newer
record revision is an update candidate, not overwrite authority. Even identical
record bytes at a new path require source/target identity, ownership, drift,
collision, reference and retirement checks. Old record locators are matched by
stable ID across the actual collection; missing or ambiguous identity blocks
dependent writes. Rendering alone does not increment a source record revision.

The split retained approval, return, evaluation, independent code-review and
strict-parity duties, delivery's full stages and escalation after two attempts
of the same failure class without meaningful progress. Actual user or
environment deadlines, cost, query and attempt caps retain their source, scope
and binding force. Agent time estimates and allocations are reassessment
checkpoints, not automatic termination or
quality-failure criteria. A critic may challenge allocation; the primary may
revise it with reasons and expected information value inside existing authority,
scope and actual caps without renewed approval for its own estimate. Earlier
usage, pending commitments and closeout reserve carry across sessions and runs.
Sufficiency depends on required evidence, material unresolved concerns, useful
additional information and acceptance; unchanged review is not repeated
endlessly. A query cap stops affected queries while permitted local analysis
continues, and a worker return does not establish whole-task completion.

For delivery, an actual cap that prevents required continuation after useful
permitted work, or a whole-task deadline with unmet work/acceptance, produces a
resource reason in the existing `blocked` state. A satisfied result does not
become blocked or partial solely because a limit was reached. Existing evaluator
results, observed coverage and genuine defects remain; unperformed checks and
spec/planning defects are not invented. Technical or resource blocking alone
does not establish a spec/planning defect. After reporting, existing human routing
governs return or unblock. Resource-block resumption requires permissible capacity and authority,
valid feature/spec boundaries and a new run linked to its predecessor, carrying
usage, pending work and earlier limits; authorized limit changes are recorded
separately. After a technical-only local unblock, the existing run may retry as
a new attempt after the required human routing decision if the approved feature
and active execution contract remain valid. A post-run spec or planning correction
requires a new run. The final documentation pass reconciled these existing
conditions between governance and runner; these are contract checks, not runtime
execution proof.

Fixed comparison criteria, trial/stop conditions, outcomes, failed bound checks
and cumulative use are not retroactively revised by later planning. In
particular, a historical 1,440-second trial's 25.2-second overrun remains a failed
bound check; it is not a default research deadline or invalidated by the later
checkpoint distinction. Resource outcome, evidence coverage and quality verdict
remain separate.

## Outcome And Reuse

The supported lesson is limited: explicit current source-to-target mappings,
stable record identity and historical per-entry provenance make the reviewed
layout changes inspectable without resetting accepted baselines. Static and
manual rehearsals support that preservation within their recorded snapshots;
they do not establish successful automated import, consumer migration or runtime
operation. The guide and record contract remain the rule owners.

The canonical record is
`evidence/harness/adoption/harness-mapping-v10-validation.md`, listed once in the
common catalog. The primary approved public fitness after checking reusable
meaning, preserved predicates, standalone reading and exclusion of private
identities, paths, source hashes, traces and associations. Separate human case
acceptance is not requested. Source implementation, record admission, external
publication, consumer refresh and observed activation remain separate facts;
none is inferred from another. This account does not supersede the earlier
Harness Improvement adoption review or rewrite its historical subjects.
