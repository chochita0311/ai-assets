# Maintain Context Freshness Checklist

Use this as the final yes-or-no pass.

## Target And Scope

- Is the existing target explicit and unambiguous?
- Is the audit scope bounded?
- Was an initial claim and source map frozen before broad refresh?
- Was the selected claim and source map, rather than target file count, used as the audit denominator?
- Is the mode `audit`, `maintain`, or `dispose`?
- If target or persistence resolution was needed, was it kept outside this skill?
- Did every selected material claim meet the decision-sufficient stop condition?
- Are no accessible unchecked sources likely to change a material disposition or confidence?
- Does the report avoid implying whole-target completeness?

## Evidence And Judgment

- Were freshness-sensitive claims selected before broad scanning?
- Were destructive, irreversible, recovery, current-action, ownership, and preservation claims prioritized before routine aligned background?
- Was source priority chosen per claim domain?
- Was every later source opened because it could change a named finding's disposition or confidence?
- When applicable, were directly coupled reprocessing, rollback, recovery, status-closure, and target-owned freshness or disposal rules checked?
- Are checked and important unchecked sources explicit?
- Is the evidence date or observation window recorded?
- Were freshness status, lifecycle role, and action disposition kept separate?
- Does each ledger row contain one atomic claim or a section with shared evidence and classifications?
- For resources and pipelines, were declaration, configuration, deployment, population or ingestion, and actual use split when their evidence differed?
- Is lifecycle written as proposed or actual transition according to the mode?
- Were conflicts and unknowns preserved as open?
- Does each material conflict or unknown name a closure source or condition?
- Was confidence recorded for consequential dispositions?

## Preservation

- Was age, completion, inactivity, or apparent duplication rejected as sole disposal evidence?
- Were historical identifiers, timelines, decisions, and observations preserved with their scope?
- Were reusable learning, diagnosis, rollback, audit, and reproduction values considered?
- Was condensation preceded by unique-content comparison?
- Were stable and unrelated areas left unchanged?

## Approval And Action

- Did an unreviewed request begin in `audit` mode?
- Did `maintain` apply only an approved or already reviewed scope?
- Were archive and delete withheld outside `dispose` mode?
- For every proposed candidate, did ownership, coverage, future-value, and dependency proof pass?
- Before `dispose`, did authorization proof pass?
- Did the user approve the exact source path or section and exact action?
- For archive, did the user approve the exact destination, or was the action reported as not applied with the exact missing destination before entering `dispose`?
- Were link and successor checks rerun after changes?

## State And Persistence

- Did `audit` keep its ledger, review state, and proposed actions session-only unless the user explicitly requested a durable artifact and named its destination or confirmed its established owner?
- Did `maintain` write only approved content-state changes to the smallest owning documents?
- Were finding IDs, review state, approval status, action status, and other run metadata kept out of target documents?
- Did `dispose` keep approval inputs and execution status in the session report while limiting persisted effects to the exact approved operation and necessary link or successor repairs?
- Was no audit log or fallback persistence location invented?

## Reporting

- Does the report name what changed and what did not?
- Does it explain what was deliberately preserved and why?
- Are delete targets and archive source targets listed exactly, with each destination or exact missing execution input explicit?
- When disposal is discussed, does the report distinguish `proposed only — not executed`, `not applied`, `approved`, and `applied` without presenting `authorization: pending` to the user?
- Do preservation, conflict, and action sections reference stable finding IDs instead of duplicating the ledger?
- Are checked sources summarized by role, with individual files named only for material findings?
- Are routine aligned evidence and non-material source inventories grouped instead of expanded into ledger rows?
- Is every material signal surfaced during the bounded scan ledgered or explicitly excluded with a decision-relevant reason?
- Are `Result`, `Target Outcome`, and `Evidence Coverage` reported separately with the correct referents?
- If the requested mode completed correctly, did target drift, unresolved findings, or partial coverage avoid lowering `Result` by themselves?
- If scope-control intervention was needed, is `Result` no better than `PASS WITH SUGGESTIONS`?

## Mode-Aware Final Check

- In `audit`, does the report enable a safe next decision without implying that the target changed?
- In `maintain`, is the current surface clearer without making the evidence layer less trustworthy or recoverable?
- In `dispose`, did every action match exact approval and report link, dependency, and recovery status?
