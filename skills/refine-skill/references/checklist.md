# Refine Skill Checklist

## Purpose

Use this final pass in both modes. Answer yes or no; use not applicable with a reason for checks outside the declared scope. Record missing execution evidence as a gap, not as a passed behavioral check.

## Final Validation

- Are the canonical target, mode, and existing authorization clear, with audit-only requests leaving the target unchanged?
- Is the exact `v1` package and available execution evidence identified, with static evidence distinguished from observed behavior?
- Do the success checks cover the target's actual purpose rather than only file structure or wording?
- Does each material finding have evidence, a likely cause or stated uncertainty, and the correct owner?
- Are justified corrections applied within authorization, without unnecessary reconfirmation, unrelated changes, or unauthorized external actions?
- Are no-change decisions supported, core content and working behavior preserved, and rules assigned to actual owners without imposing a fixed file layout?
- Are affected examples, metadata, templates, and executable resources aligned, with applicable static and resource checks completed or their gaps named?
- Do reported comparisons identify relevant revision, input, model, tool, and context differences and any author intervention?
- Does each claimed forward-test or rerun contain an actual task execution, with independence and comparability stated accurately?
- Where behavior is claimed improved, are the original failure and relevant successful controls checked, including authorized completion when restrictions changed?
- Are invocation changes checked against realistic intended and out-of-scope requests, or explicitly left as an evidence gap?
- Are `Result`, `Evidence Coverage`, highest evidence level, regressions, and unresolved follow-ups reported without overstating stability?
- Are disposable artifacts cleaned only within known task ownership, retained paths explained, and durable references free of temporary-path dependencies?
