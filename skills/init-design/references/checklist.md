# Init Design Checklist

Use this as the final binary validation pass after drafting the constitution and design-document governance.

## Source And Compatibility

- Is the starting source-set type explicit?
- Is source priority explicit, including the anchor source when several artifacts exist?
- Are existing durable rules and the authorized reset scope respected?
- Are selected visual sources distinguished from discovery leads, external inspiration, and derived analyses, with locators and observation scope?
- Were source contradictions resolved or preserved as open instead of blended?
- Are inaccessible, cached, truncated, or reconstructed sources qualified without pretending they were directly observed?
- Do product entities, roles, states, validation, and device constraints inform the durable rules?

## Constitution

- Does the constitution stand alone as durable design law?
- Are design DNA, primitive tokens, semantic tokens, layout rules, and core component rules explicit?
- Are material token values traceable, with observed/reported/inferred/proposed bases and normalization reasons kept distinct from adoption?
- Do semantic aliases resolve, and do typography tuples preserve known associations and actual text-bearing elements rather than pairing independent lists or substituting container styles, with material role changes explained?
- Do material foreground/background pairs satisfy the stated contrast rules on their actual host surfaces across required component variants and states, with scoped adaptations or usage restrictions recorded and no incompatible inherited hover mapping?
- Do rules preserve intended character beyond colors, without importing unrelated brand assets, product fields, or workflows?
- Are required states, responsive behavior, accessibility, and font/asset fallbacks addressed without claiming unobserved behavior was verified?
- Are screen families limited to durable product structure supported by the source set?
- Did the result avoid forcing a fixed screen count?
- Can additional supported screens be built without inventing new color, radius, spacing, component, or tone systems?
- Are immediate tasks, sequencing, and temporary uncertainty absent?

## Governance

- Does the governance document name the actual creative source when one exists?
- Does it distinguish creative intent, durable law, and optional active planning?
- Is the update owner clear for each kind of future change?
- Are one-off experiments prevented from becoming durable law automatically?
- Are external reference changes prevented from silently changing locked rules, and are any derived exports assigned an owner and synchronization rule?

## Evidence And Handoff

- Can another agent evaluate the outputs without conversation-only assumptions?
- Are unsupported inferences and remaining risks explicit?
- Are actual rendered checks distinguished from source-based walkthroughs, with gaps and completion scope stated?
- Are documents usable without unfilled placeholders, and do any requested exports agree with the adopted rules?
- Are follow-up actions kept in the response or handed to a separate planning workflow?

## Final Question

- After a long break, can the project recover what is locked, why it is locked, which screen families are supported, and where future design changes belong?
