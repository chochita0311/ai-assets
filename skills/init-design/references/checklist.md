# Init Design Checklist

Use this as the final binary validation pass after drafting the constitution and design-document governance.

## Source And Compatibility

- Is the starting source-set type explicit?
- Is source priority explicit, including the anchor source when several artifacts exist?
- Were source contradictions resolved or preserved as open instead of blended?
- Do product entities, roles, states, validation, and device constraints inform the durable rules?

## Constitution

- Does the constitution stand alone as durable design law?
- Are design DNA, primitive tokens, semantic tokens, layout rules, and core component rules explicit?
- Are screen families limited to durable product structure supported by the source set?
- Did the result avoid forcing a fixed screen count?
- Can additional supported screens be built without inventing new color, radius, spacing, component, or tone systems?
- Are immediate tasks, sequencing, and temporary uncertainty absent?

## Governance

- Does the governance document name the actual creative source when one exists?
- Does it distinguish creative intent, durable law, and optional active planning?
- Is the update owner clear for each kind of future change?
- Are one-off experiments prevented from becoming durable law automatically?

## Evidence And Handoff

- Can another agent evaluate the outputs without conversation-only assumptions?
- Are unsupported inferences and remaining risks explicit?
- Are follow-up actions kept in the response or handed to a separate planning workflow?

## Final Question

- After a long break, can the project recover what is locked, why it is locked, which screen families are supported, and where future design changes belong?
